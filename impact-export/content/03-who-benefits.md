# Who benefits — six audiences downstream of one engine

### Metrological Assistant
Stops hand-checking arithmetic and re-typing after every review comment. Enters a reading once; the pass/fail and the mpe band it was checked against are on screen immediately.
**Win:** Fewer round-trips per report.

### AD/DD & Director
Reviews a computed verdict and a signed chain instead of re-deriving numbers from a spreadsheet they did not build. Deficiencies attach to the record instead of spawning a new file.
**Win:** Review time, not calculation time.

### Manufacturer / applicant
A wrong band boundary on a multi-interval scale — the #1 thing the PS says manual spreadsheets get wrong — no longer costs a fresh ₹25,000 application and months of resubmission (Rule 10).
**Win:** Fewer false rejections.

### DoCA / Director (LM), nationally
Rule 4(9)'s annual return — models tested, fees, time per test — is a dashboard query instead of a manual compilation across 6 RRSLs and 40 GATCs with a 10x workload spread between them (Guwahati 3–8/yr vs Varanasi 40–76/yr).
**Win:** Uniform data, first time it exists.

### The public buying on a scale
Every retail weighing instrument in India passed (or should have passed) this exact test chain before sale. A faster, more consistent gate means fewer non-conforming instruments reaching a shop counter undetected.
**Win:** Downstream consumer protection.

### India's OIML standing
Since Sept 2023 India is the 13th country authorised to issue OIML pattern-approval certificates — the R-76 report format is now an international deliverable, not just a domestic one. A tool that outputs both profiles from one dataset (Rule 7(1)(a)) is what keeps that credible.
**Win:** International-facing consistency.
